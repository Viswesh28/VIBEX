# DMCA Policy & Takedown Procedure

**Last updated:** 2026-09-22

This document explains how rights holders can request the removal of content
or links associated with this project, and states plainly what this project
does and does not host.

---

## What this repository hosts

**This repository contains no copyrighted music, artwork, or lyrics.**

VIBEX is a front-end interface plus an API gateway. It:

- **does not** store, bundle, cache to disk, seed, or redistribute any audio
  file, album artwork, or lyric text;
- **does not** maintain any index or catalogue of media files;
- **does not** operate any server that stores media;
- resolves playback URLs **on demand, at the moment of playback**, by querying
  a third-party API, and streams them directly from that service's own CDN to
  the end user's browser.

The only copyrighted-adjacent material that may appear in the repository is
the reference imagery in `image-search/`, which is excluded from all builds
and which maintainers are expected to delete before public distribution.

## Reporting infringement

If you are a copyright owner, or an agent authorised to act on behalf of one,
and you believe that material associated with this project infringes your
copyright, send a notification containing **all** of the following, as required
by 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3):

1. **Identification of the copyrighted work** claimed to be infringed, or, if
   multiple works are covered by a single notification, a representative list.
2. **Identification of the material** claimed to be infringing, with
   information reasonably sufficient to locate it — a URL, file path, or commit
   reference where possible.
3. **Your contact information** — name, postal address, telephone number, and
   email address.
4. **A statement of good-faith belief** that use of the material in the manner
   complained of is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the
   law.
5. **A statement, made under penalty of perjury**, that the information in the
   notification is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or are
   authorised to act on the owner's behalf.
6. **Your physical or electronic signature.**

### Where to send it

Open a **GitHub issue** on this repository, or contact the maintainer through

<https://github.com/Viswesh28/VIBEX>

## What happens next

Maintainers aim to acknowledge valid notices **within 72 hours** and will take
one or more of the following actions, depending on the nature of the complaint:

- Remove the referenced material, link, or code from the repository.
- Disable the affected feature or endpoint.
- Take the repository private or delete it entirely.
- Add a specific blocklist entry for the identified work.

Because this project hosts no media, many complaints about *playback* of a
specific recording are properly addressed to the service that serves the audio
(JioSaavn) rather than to this repository. Maintainers will say so plainly and
promptly if that is the case.

## Counter-notification

If material you contributed was removed as a result of a mistake or
misidentification, you may submit a counter-notification containing the
elements set out in 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3), including your name, address,
telephone number, a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a
good-faith belief the material was removed in error, and your consent to the
jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address.

---

## Repeat infringer policy

Maintainers will, in appropriate circumstances, disable or remove
contributions from users who are repeat infringers.

## Jurisdictional note

This policy is written to be compatible with the notice-and-takedown
frameworks of the United States (DMCA, 17 U.S.C. § 512) and the European Union
(Directive 2000/31/EC, Art. 14; Digital Services Act). It is offered as a
good-faith mechanism for rights holders regardless of where a claim arises.
